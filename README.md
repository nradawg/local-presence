**Use this when** you run a business with a van, a shop, or a service area, and
you want more calls out of the listing you already have.

## The rule

> Check the live profile before advising on it, and check the phone number
> before anything else.

Almost every local recommendation you have read was written without looking at
the profile in question. Yours is public and readable in a browser with no
login, so there is no excuse for guessing at it.

## What I check first, because it is free

Search your business name plus your city and read the profile that comes back.
That settles most of this without asking you a thing:

- Does a profile exist at all, and is it verified.
- What the primary category currently is.
- Whether the name field has extra words in it.
- Whether an address shows for a business with no storefront.
- Review count and the date of the most recent one.
- Whether the hours are plausible for today.
- Whether the phone number rings a person. **Call it.** Highest leverage check
  on the page, and nobody runs it.

## The things that actually matter, and the rules on them

Verify these against Google's own page rather than mine, because they change:
<https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177>

**Primary category.** Pick the fewest categories that describe the core
business, and the most specific one available. The category describes what the
business *is*, not a list of everything it does. This field does more than any
other single edit, and a wrong one is common.

**The name field.** Your real world business name only. No taglines, no city,
no keywords, no phone number. Stuffing it is a violation and the profile is
what you lose.

**Service area, if you drive to customers.** One profile, for your central
office, with the address hidden. Google's guidance is that the area generally
should not run more than about two hours of driving from your base, and there
are limits on how many areas and how broad they can be. No P.O. boxes, no
virtual offices.

**Reviews.** Ask everyone the same way, every time. Asking only the happy ones,
discouraging negative ones, or filtering before you ask is gating, it is
against policy, and it risks the profile. Reply to every review in public,
including the bad one.

**Hours and photos.** Wrong hours cost you the call. Photos of the actual work
beat stock anything.

**The feature set changes.** Do not plan around a Business Profile feature you
read about in an article. Open the dashboard and see which fields exist right
now. Features get added and retired on Google's schedule, and advice ages fast.

## What you see

```
LOCAL AUDIT

Q  Does a verified profile exist?
A  Yes. CHECKED, it returns for "name + Utah County" and shows
   hours and a phone number.

Q  What is the primary category?
A  "Cleaners." CHECKED, that is what the profile displays.
   This is probably wrong. "Window cleaning service" exists as a
   category and is far more specific. Highest value fix here.

Q  Is the name field clean?
A  No. It reads "Name, Window Cleaning Utah County."
   CHECKED. That is keyword stuffing in the name field and it is
   the kind of thing profiles get suspended for.

Q  Does the phone number reach a person?
A  GUESSED. I cannot place a call. LOAD BEARING. Call it from a
   number you do not own and see what happens.

Q  Is the address hidden?
A  No, a residential address is showing. CHECKED.
   If you have no storefront customers visit, that should be a
   hidden address with a service area instead.

Q  Reviews?
A  Six, most recent four months ago. CHECKED from the profile.
   Nothing here suggests gating, and do not start.

Q  Are the hours right?
A  GUESSED. Profile says Mon through Fri. Your booking page says
   Tue through Sat. One of them is wrong and it is costing calls.

7 answers. 2 guessed, both load bearing. 2 live problems found.
Fix order: name field, then category, then hide the address.
```

## Limits

I cannot place a phone call, and the phone test is the most valuable item on
this list. You have to do that one.

I cannot see your dashboard. Everything above comes from the public profile, so
insights and message history stay invisible to me unless you paste them.

I have no ranking numbers. No "this moves you up N positions," no traffic
estimates, no timelines. Local ranking is not published, and anybody quoting
you a figure is guessing with confidence. I also cannot fix a suspended
profile, reinstatement is a separate process with Google.

**The trap:** a naive version of this recycles tactics from old articles. Seed
your own Q&A section, post weekly to rank, buy citation packages, ask your
happy customers for reviews. That last one is a policy violation that can cost
you the profile, and at least one of the others may be a feature that no longer
exists. Meanwhile the real problem is usually dull and in plain sight: the
wrong category, a stuffed name, or a number that rings where nobody picks up.

## How this one knows what it knows

Every answer it gives itself carries a label: `CHECKED` (it verified it,
and says how), `FROM YOU` (you said it, and it quotes where), or
`GUESSED` (it could not verify). Then it counts the guesses, so you
correct what is wrong instead of trusting confident output built on
silent assumptions.

> Never ask for something you can check.
> Never assume something you cannot check.
